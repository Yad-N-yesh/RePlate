FIGHT FOOD WASTE WITH REPLATE

RePlate is an website that connects people with unsold food from local grocery stores at roughly a half of the original price. Businesses put up their 'About to expire' at the end of the day, and customers reserve and pick it up through the Website. The result is less food in the bin, extra revenue for businesses, and a cheaper meal for the customer.

🌱 OUR BUSINESS MODEL

We operate a win-win-win business model: for people, profit and the planet. RePlate's focus is to help save good food from going to waste. By doing so, we help businesses unlock revenue from their product, help consumers enjoy good food at great value for money.

📈 INCREMENTAL VALUE

By partnering with RePlate, you can sell your surplus food at a discount to a whole new group of customers who care about sustainability and saving money. This not only generates revenue from the bags sold, but also increases footfall and customer loyalty.
